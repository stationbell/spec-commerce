# Code citations used in the demo (values and references only)

Prepared for the Westgate Data Center demo. These are the code rows the customer supplies; the
product page checks the ones that are product facts and marks the rest as depending on the
installation.

| Ref | Source | Requirement (paraphrased) | Value | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-1 | NFPA 10 (2022), Table 6.2.1.1 | Light (low) hazard occupancy: minimum rated single extinguisher; maximum travel distance | 2-A; 75 ft | Extinguisher rating (product fact) |
| C-2 | NFPA 10 (2022), Chapter 5 (Class C hazards) | Extinguishers for fires involving energized electrical equipment carry a Class C rating | C | Extinguisher rating (product fact) |
| C-3 | NFPA 10 (2022), 6.1.3.8.1 | Extinguishers with gross weight ≤ 40 lb: top of extinguisher not more than 5 ft above the floor | 60 in | Installation, not a product fact |
| C-4 | 2010 ADA Standards, 307.2 | Objects with leading edges between 27 in and 80 in above the floor protrude no more than 4 in into the circulation path | 4 in, given installed height and location | Installation context; the project spec §2.3.E applies the 4 in limit to the cabinet |

Hazard classification for C-1 (light hazard) is the customer's determination for the data hall and
support rooms. The governing edition and the authority having jurisdiction decide applicability;
the page reports how the product compares with the row as supplied.
